## Notes

- **Strongest for volume and low competition:** "seo audit service", "landing page design", "small business website design". These have 5,000 searches each and low competition.
- **Good fit, low volume:** "visual identity design", "brand naming agency", "instagram profile audit". Less traffic, but the buyer is close to ordering.
- **High bid, high value:** "google ads setup" and "conversion rate optimization audit". Bids are high, so these are buyer-intent terms.
- **Not used:** "free seo audit", "free site audit" and "free content audit". They match a free offer we do not sell. Do not target them.
- **Content package:** the search data is thin for this offer. "social media content package" is the best match.
