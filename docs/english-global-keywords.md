# English keyword list (worldwide)

Target: English-speaking businesses in Europe, the US, Australia, Canada and other markets. No single-country targeting.

**Important:** This list is based on service names and common phrasing, not search volumes. No keyword tool is connected. Before choosing final targets, check volume and competition in Google Search Console (queries for the English pages) and Google Keyword Planner.

## Priority rule
- **Primary:** one main phrase per page, in the title, H1 and description.
- **Secondary:** 2 to 4 related phrases, used naturally in H2s and body copy.
- Do not repeat "worldwide" in every title. The English site is global by default.
